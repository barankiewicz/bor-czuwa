# Play listing: category and tags (proposal)

For the owner to set in Play Console. Nothing here is submitted.

**Category.** Application type "Application", category "Tools" is the closest fit for an app that
measures and prepares a document. "Maps & Navigation" is the alternative if the map step is what
the listing leads with; the store description leads with the notice, so "Tools" is proposed.

**Tags.** Play Console offers up to five tags from a list that depends on the category, so choose
from what the Console shows rather than from this file. The meaning to look for, in order: utility,
location, maps, safety, documents. Avoid any tag about hunting itself, which would put the app in
front of the people it reports.

**Contact and policy.** The privacy policy URL is the landing's, `/prywatnosc/` and `/en/privacy/`
on the landing host. The support address waits for the project mailbox (human step 03).

**Title and short description.** Already in `metadata/android/*/`. The landing's title and meta
description proposals for search are in ticket 14; keep the Play short description as it is.
