Heard a shot near home, seen a person with a gun, or found a wounded animal? Bór czuwa lets you record a hunting incident in Poland and prepare a notice to the authority responsible for that place.

Put your safety first. Do not approach, follow anyone or interfere with a hunt. You can leave and finish the record later.

One tap saves a draft with the time and available location, without internet. Later, choose what happened: a shot, a person with a gun, remains in the forest, a wounded animal or poaching. You can add a note and, for events other than shots, photos.

Once you have a connection, the server calculates measurements for the place and time. The app shows their uncertainty and the relevant rules. Measurements do not decide guilt.

You can prepare separate notices for the police, Państwowa Straż Łowiecka, the gmina and, where applicable, a national park or RDOŚ. Send the notice from your own email or save a PDF. Choose whether to sign it. The recipient sees the email address you send from. Your report is counted even if you send no notice.

No account is required. Notes, photos and signature details stay on your phone until you choose to send them to a recipient. The server report contains the place, time, event type and information needed for measurements. For a report made from home, the exact home location stays on your phone. You can withdraw a report in the app.

The interface supports Polish and English. Notices are always written in Polish. The app's source code is open.

This is not an emergency service. Recording a report does not bring help. Call 112 in an emergency. The app is independent and does not represent the police or any other authority.
