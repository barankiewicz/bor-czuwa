## What the app is

Bór czuwa lets you record an incident connected with hunting: the place, the time and the event
type. The server then measures the distances from zabudowania mieszkalne (homes) and obszary
chronione (protected areas). On that basis the app composes a notice to the authorities you
choose. You send it from your own mailbox or file a signed one as a PDF through ePUAP or
e-Doręczenia. It is run by Alicja Barankiewicz, bor-czuwa@pm.me. Using it is free, needs no
account and works on Android or in a browser. You can stop at any time.

By using the app you accept these terms. Do not send unlawful content. You are the sender of the
notice, with its note and photos. What happens to data is described in the
[privacy policy](/legal/privacy/v1/).

## Report what happened

Knowingly notifying the authorities of an offence that was not committed is itself an offence
(Art. 238 of the Kodeks karny, the Polish Criminal Code), and so is falsely accusing a specific
person (Art. 234). A report made in good faith, about what really happened, is not an offence,
even if it turns out that no breach took place. [Brzmienie do potwierdzenia przez prawników.]

## Measurements

The measurements are distances and areas worked out from public spatial data, which can be out of
date or only indicative. They carry a margin that comes from the accuracy of the position. They
describe a place and a time, not whether anyone broke the law. We do not guarantee that they are
free of errors.

## Publication and exclusion

Reports will be published on a public map with an approximate place and the date, without notes or
photos. The operator of the service may exclude a report that looks false or mass-generated. On the
map, an exclusion will be shown with its reason and can be reversed.

Anyone a report concerns can ask for it to be removed from the map by writing to
bor-czuwa@pm.me. The details are in the privacy policy.

## No guarantees

A report does not bring help. We do not know whether a notice arrived or what its recipient did
with it. The service may sometimes be unavailable. The report then waits on the phone, and a
notice can be composed without the server.
